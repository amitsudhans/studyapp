Now step into the workhorse sequence of algebra, finance, and physics inside Section Eight Point Four: ARITHMETIC PROGRESSIONS, abbreviated simply as APs! Suppose a mountain climber climbs three hundred metres higher every single hour, or a savings jar adds fifty rupees every week, or a hot oven cools by four degrees every minute! Look closely at the exact gap between any two consecutive terms: always subtract the earlier term from the very next term, and the difference stays COMPLETELY CONSTANT! An ARITHMETIC PROGRESSION is a sequence in which every term after the first is obtained by ADDING a fixed constant number—called the COMMON DIFFERENCE d—to the preceding term! Denote the first term by letter a! Then the general form of EVERY AP reads smoothly: a, a plus d, a plus two d, a plus three d, continuing endlessly! Watch out for negative and zero differences too! When terms decrease steadily like twenty, sixteen, twelve, eight, compute d equals sixteen minus twenty equals NEGATIVE FOUR—never reverse the subtraction order! When every term stays completely identical like seven, seven, seven, seven, common difference d equals ZERO, giving a completely flat constant horizontal arithmetic progression whose terms never rise or fall at any position!

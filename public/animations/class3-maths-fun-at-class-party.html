10 Questions Multi-Choice Quiz
1

If a study desk edge measures 7 large handspans, how many smaller handspans are expected for the same edge?

💡 Hint: Smaller units cover less physical distance with each step.
2

A student measures a tabletop as 9 handspans and a friend measures it as 11 handspans. What explains this difference?

💡 Hint: More spans mean each individual span is shorter.
3

A rectangular green board takes 8 decorative paper strings across the top and 5 along the side. What is its total perimeter?

💡 Hint: A rectangle has two equal horizontal sides and two equal vertical sides.
4

When measuring a table border with paper strings, which practice guarantees an accurate measurement?

💡 Hint: Units must line up consecutively in a continuous line.
5

On a costume mask, the left curled moustache is 9 cm long. By mirror symmetry, what must be the length of the right curl?

💡 Hint: Bilateral symmetry means the two opposite halves are identical in length.
6

A string of festive party lights has 20 small bulbs spaced 5 cm apart. What is the distance from the 1st to the 2nd bulb?

💡 Hint: Look at the spacing given between consecutive bulbs.
7

Why is a flexible cotton cord better than a wooden ruler for finding the distance around a round tree trunk?

💡 Hint: Think about the shape of a curved surface.
8

If 1 decorative paper ribbon measures 15 cm, how long is a border made of 4 identical ribbons placed end-to-end?

💡 Hint: Multiply the length of 1 ribbon by 4.
9

Which of these is a non-standard unit of length?

💡 Hint: Standard units have fixed, universal values defined by international agreement.
10

A bulletin board perimeter requires 18 paper strips. If the top and bottom need 6 strips each, how many strips does each side need?

💡 Hint: Total = 18. Subtract top and bottom, then divide the remainder by 2 sides.

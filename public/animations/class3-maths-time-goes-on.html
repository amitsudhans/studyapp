If July 1 falls on a Wednesday, what day of the week is July 15?

Which of the following months has exactly 30 days?

💡 Hint: Remember the rhyme: Thirty days hath September, April, June, and November.

A standard non-leap year has how many total weeks and extra days?

💡 Hint: Divide 365 by 7.
5

If a date is on Sunday, July 7, what is the date of the very next Sunday?

How many months in a single calendar year have 31 days?

💡 Hint: List them: Jan, Mar, May, Jul, Aug, Oct, Dec.
7

In a leap year, how many days does February contain?

💡 Hint: One extra day is added to February every 4 years.
8

If school holidays begin on June 1 and last for 4 weeks, on what date do they end?

💡 Hint: 4 weeks equals 4 × 7 = 28 days.

What is the day of the week if tomorrow is Friday?

💡 Hint: Which day comes immediately before Friday?
10

Which national holiday is celebrated on October 2 each year?

💡 Hint: This day honors the birth of Mahatma Gandhi.

💡 Hint: Subtract 36 from 64.

💡 Hint: Order from smallest year to largest year.

A certificate was issued on 20/08/2020. How many years passed until 20/08/2024?

💡 Hint: Subtract 2020 from 2024.

If a child celebrated their 7th birthday in 2023, which birthday will they celebrate in 2027?

💡 Hint: 2027 is 4 years after 2023. Add 4 to 7.

What information is NOT typically found on a standard birth certificate?

💡 Hint: A birth certificate is issued at birth, before school starts.

How many hours does the hour hand take to make one complete revolution of the dial?

If the minute hand moves from 2 to 5, how many minutes have elapsed?

💡 Hint: The hand moves through 3 intervals of 5 minutes.
7

Which of the following is equivalent to 'quarter past nine'?

💡 Hint: A quarter of an hour is 15 minutes.
8

When the clock shows 11:30, where is the hour hand positioned?

💡 Hint: At half past, the hour hand has traveled halfway to the next hour.
9

How many total minutes are in 2 and a half hours?

💡 Hint: 2 hours = 120 minutes, plus half an hour (30 minutes).

At what time do the hour hand and minute hand point in opposite directions to form a straight line?

Celestial Mechanics

Earth Axial Rotation: The Science of Day & Night

Rotate planet Earth counter-clockwise (West to East) to see why the Sun appears to transit East to West.

DAY
NIGHT
1 Full Rotation = 24 Hours
The Moving Train Analogy

When your train pulls forward out of a station, the trees and platform appear to rush backwards in reverse. Similarly, because Earth spins smoothly from West to East, the stationary Sun appears to move from East to West!

Scientific Truth: The Sun is stationary at the solar system center; Earth's spin creates the day-night cycle.

Step One: ALIGN both numbers cleanly by their rightmost ones digits, padding any shorter number on the left with leading zeros so both have equal digit length d! Imagine tiling an m-by-n rectangular floor (say eighteen by twelve) using identical square tiles of the largest possible side length d!

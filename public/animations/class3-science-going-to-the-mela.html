Challenge 3

10 Questions Multi-Choice Quiz
1

Which scientific principle explains why a spinning top (lattoo) remains upright on its narrow tip?

💡 Hint: Rapid rotation produces gyroscopic stability that resists falling over.
2

Why are indigenous toys crafted from seasoned wood and clay more eco-friendly than cheap plastic toys?

💡 Hint: Wood and clay decompose naturally and contain no toxic battery chemicals.
3

What force acts towards the center of a circular path to keep horses on a rotating merry-go-round?

💡 Hint: The inward-seeking force in circular motion is called centripetal force.
4

Why do passengers on a giant Ferris wheel experience a momentary feeling of lightness at the highest point?

💡 Hint: At the apex, downward inertia slightly offsets normal contact force from the seat.
5

What mechanical advantage does a traditional string puppet control cross (marionette controller) provide?

💡 Hint: Tilting the wooden cross pulls specific strings attached to hands, head, or feet.
6

What traditional acoustic instruments typically accompany a regional Indian puppet performance?

💡 Hint: Traditional folk shows use natural drums, cymbals, and reed whistles.
7

What is the primary reason ride operators enforce strict height and age limits on amusement rides?

💡 Hint: Restraint bars must fit snugly to prevent smaller children from slipping out.
8

How does aerodynamic lift cause a paper pinwheel (phirki) to spin when held against the wind?

💡 Hint: Curved blades deflect air, producing rotational force around the central pin.
9

What cultural and moral function do traditional puppet dramas fulfill in society?

💡 Hint: Folklore dramas teach moral lessons through heroic legends and fables.
10

What should a visitor do immediately if they notice a loose bolt or frayed cable on an amusement ride?

💡 Hint: Reporting hazards to operators prevents potential accidents.

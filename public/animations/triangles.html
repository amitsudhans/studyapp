Basic Proportionality Theorem

Master one of Class Ten mathematics most celebrated proofs: Theorem Six Point One, the Basic Proportionality Theorem, famously honored as Thales Theorem! Listen carefully to the exact theorem statement: If a line is drawn parallel to one side of a triangle intersecting the other two sides in distinct points, then the other two sides are divided in the exact same ratio! Consider triangle ABC where line DE runs parallel to side BC, cutting side AB at D and side AC at E! We must prove AD over DB equals AE over EC! Follow this crystal clear four-step area proof! Step One: join B to E and C to D, plus draw altitudes EN perpendicular to AB and DM perpendicular to AC! Step Two: Compare triangles ADE and BDE sharing equal altitude EN: area ADE over area BDE simplifies cleanly to AD over DB! Step Three: Compare triangles ADE and CDE sharing equal altitude DM: area ADE over area CDE simplifies cleanly to AE over EC! Step Four: Notice triangles BDE and CDE stand on the same base DE between the same parallels DE and BC, making their areas strictly EQUAL, proving AD over DB equals AE over EC!

📐

Theorem 6.1 Lab (BPT / Thales): Parallel Cut DE ∥ BC

AD/DB = AE/EC = 1.00
Slide Parallel Line DE up & down (DE always stays ∥ BC):50% down AB & AC
AD/DB = ar(ADE)/ar(BDE) = 1.00 | AE/EC = ar(ADE)/ar(CDE) = 1.00
Same Base DE between DE || BC => ar(BDE) = ar(CDE) => AD/DB = AE/EC ALWAYS!

BPT Extensions (+1 Corollary) & Trapezium Diagonals (Ex 6.2 Q9)

AO/BO = CO/DO
Start from BPT (DE || BC): AD / DB = AE / EC
1) Add 1 to both sides: (AD + DB)/DB = (AE + EC)/EC => AB / DB = AC / EC
2) Flip & Add 1: DB/AD + 1 = EC/AE + 1 => AB / AD = AC / AE => AD / AB = AE / AC
Trapezium ABCD (AB || DC), diagonals meet at O. Draw EO || AB || DC:
1) In triangle ADC (EO || DC): AE / ED = AO / OC
2) In triangle DAB (EO || AB): AE / ED = BO / OD
3) Equating AE/ED: AO / OC = BO / OD => AO / BO = CO / DO! (Proved!)

In geometry, whenever a forward theorem shines bright, immediately flip the logical arrow backwards and test its CONVERSE! Welcome to Theorem Six Point Two, the Converse of Basic Proportionality Theorem! Here is its crystal clear theorem statement: If a line divides any two sides of a triangle in the exact same ratio, then that dividing line MUST run strictly parallel to the third side! Suppose inside triangle ABC we are GIVEN AD over DB equals AE over EC, and we must prove DE is parallel to BC! How do mathematicians prove this backwards result cleanly without guessing? By using a sleek Proof by Contradiction! Assume temporarily for argument sake that DE is NOT parallel to BC! Then through point D, Euclidean geometry lets us draw ONE true parallel line DE prime parallel to side BC, meeting side AC at some distinct point E prime! By forward BPT on DE prime parallel to BC, AD over DB equals AE prime over E prime C! Comparing against our given AD over DB equals AE over EC forces AE over EC to equal AE prime over E prime C! Adding one cleanly to both sides forces E and E prime to coincide completely, proving DE parallel to BC!

Supplementary RHS Criterion & Right-Triangle Altitude Split

RHS + △ABC ~ △ACD ~ △CBD
Right △ABC (∠B=90°) & Right △PQR (∠Q=90°): Hypotenuse AC/PR = Leg AB/PQ = k
By Pythagoras: BC = sqrt(AC^2 - AB^2) = k * sqrt(PR^2 - PQ^2) = k * QR -> BC / QR = k!
All 3 side ratios match (k) -> △ABC ~ △PQR by RHS (reducing cleanly to SSS)!
△ACD ~ △CBD => Altitude CD = sqrt(4 x 9) = 6.00 cm!

Theorem 1.1: Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic (Gauss)

Every composite number can be expressed (factorised) as a product of primes, and this factorisation is unique, apart from the order in which the prime factors occur. Anchors 4ⁿ/6ⁿ digit tests & HCF × LCM = a × b.
